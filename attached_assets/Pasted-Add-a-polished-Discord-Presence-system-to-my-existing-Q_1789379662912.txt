==================================================
RECONNECT HANDLING
==================================================

Discord may disconnect/reconnect.

After reconnect:

- Do not create multiple presence timers.
- Cancel/replace stale timers.
- Restore the appropriate current presence.
- Respect security lockdown state.
- Respect service-health state.
- Resume rotation safely.

There must NEVER be multiple presence rotation loops running simultaneously.


==================================================
SHUTDOWN
==================================================

Cleanly stop the presence rotation timer during graceful shutdown.

Do not leave unnecessary intervals/timeouts.


==================================================
